# Development Standards Plane and Observatory

The Development Standards Plane converts development guidance into a fixed-denominator catalog of mandatory, conditional, preference, and recommendation obligations. Context triggers decide applicability. Static checks, targeted tests, courts, qualification, witnesses, and reviews remain distinct check roles so a lightweight check cannot silently stand in for a stronger one.

Each check result binds its subject root, evidence root, and witness root. Mandatory or active conditional failures refuse; unresolved mandatory checks hold; preference and recommendation results remain advisory. The denominator never shrinks because a check is inconvenient.

The Development Observatory is a read-only projection over standards evaluations, Pippi decisions, launcher states, constitutional exceptions, and canonical journal heads. Its `available_actions` are navigation suggestions with `authorized: false`. Human, agent, and forensic projections share the same state root.

Use reminders at semantic boundaries: subject binding, planning, before effect authorization, after witness capture, before acceptance, after recovery, and at handoff. Avoid token-count reminders and repeated warnings that do not correspond to a changed obligation.
