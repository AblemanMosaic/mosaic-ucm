# MosaiCOS M1 interface

The stable UCM surface verifies the exact M0 evidence envelope and derives four
explicitly unbound M1 roles: MosaiCOS foundation, Genesis seed, target profile, and
Ethernet bootstrap adapter. It also records the host/native boundary and all open
obligations.

`PREPARE_MOSAICOS_M1` produces preparation only. `VERIFY_MOSAICOS_M1_PREPARATION`
rechecks its root and the unbound ceiling. Neither surface selects hardware, grants
network access, admits a target, proves native correspondence, or earns M1 acceptance.

The exact M0 fixture is retained because it is small and is the evidence subject for
this interface. Deeper native subsystem artifacts remain external until supplied and
bound through a later, separately reviewed correspondence step.
